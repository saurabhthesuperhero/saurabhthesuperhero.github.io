AdGraph

AdGraph is an Android app by Saikaizen Innovations that shows your own AdMob earnings and reports on your phone: today's earnings, month pace, apps, ad units, countries and mediation, plus a home-screen widget.

It signs in with Google and asks for read-only AdMob access ("See your AdMob data"). It cannot change ads, settings or payments. There is no AdGraph server: your reports go straight from Google to your device.
