AdGraph

AdGraph is an Android app by Saikaizen Innovations that shows your own AdMob earnings and reports on your phone: today's earnings, month pace, apps, ad units, countries and mediation, several accounts, plus a home-screen widget.

It signs in with Google and asks for read-only AdMob access ("See your AdMob data"). It cannot change ads, settings or payments. There is no AdGraph server: your reports go straight from Google to your device.

The app is free with ads; AdGraph Pro (monthly, yearly or lifetime, no free trial) removes ads. AdGraph is an independent app, not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.
