AdGraph privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. AdGraph is made by Saikaizen Innovations.

What AdGraph does

AdGraph shows your own AdMob reports on your phone. You sign in with Google and allow one permission: "See your AdMob data" (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admob.readonly). AdGraph can read reports, apps and ad units. It cannot change ads, settings or payments.

What data goes where

  • Your AdMob reports go directly from Google's AdMob API to the app on your device. AdGraph has no server and we never receive, store or see your data.
  • Reports, settings (goals, markers, theme) and a few readings of today's total are saved only on your device so the app opens quickly and works offline. Uninstalling the app or "Clear saved reports" removes them.
  • Sign-in is handled by Google. AdGraph does not see your password.
  • AdGraph has no ads, no analytics SDK and no tracking.

Google API data

AdGraph's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to show you your own reports.

Removing access

Sign out in Settings, and remove AdGraph at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Contact

Questions: use the developer contact on AdGraph's Google Play page.
