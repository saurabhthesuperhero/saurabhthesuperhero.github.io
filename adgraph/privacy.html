AdGraph privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026. AdGraph: Earnings for AdMob ("AdGraph", package com.saikaizen.adgraph) is made by Saikaizen Innovations ("we"). AdGraph is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

What AdGraph does

AdGraph shows your own AdMob reports on your phone. You sign in with Google and allow one permission: "See your AdMob data" (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admob.readonly). AdGraph can only read reports, apps and ad units. It cannot change ads, settings or payments. You can also use a demo with made-up data without signing in.

Your AdMob data and Google account

  • Your AdMob reports go directly from Google's AdMob API to the app on your device. AdGraph has no server: we never receive, store or see your reports, publisher IDs or earnings.
  • Your Google account email is used on your device only, to show which account is open and to switch between accounts. It is not sent to us. Sign-in is handled by Google; AdGraph never sees your password.
  • Reports, settings (goals, theme, saved accounts) and a few readings of today's total are saved only on your device so the app opens quickly and works offline. Signing out, "Clear saved reports" or uninstalling removes them.

Google API data (Limited Use)

AdGraph's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. AdMob data is used only to show you your own reports in the app. It is never used for ads, never sold, never shared with the ad networks or analytics below, and never read by humans.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob and its mediation partners Meta Audience Network and Liftoff (Vungle). These ad SDKs may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from IP address), app interactions and diagnostics to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, AdGraph asks for consent with Google's consent tool (UMP) before ads load; you can change your choice under Settings > Privacy options. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Policies: Google, Meta, Liftoff.

Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to count app events (for example "demo opened", "paywall viewed", "ad loaded") and to receive crash reports with device model, OS version and app version. These never include your AdMob reports, earnings, publisher IDs or email. See Firebase privacy.

Purchases (AdGraph Pro)

AdGraph Pro is bought through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment; we receive only a purchase record (product, order ID, status) to unlock Pro. We never see your card or payment details.

Children

AdGraph is a business tool for app publishers aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children.

Removing access and data

Sign out in the app, and remove AdGraph at myaccount.google.com/connections. Since we hold no account or reports on a server, there is nothing to delete on our side. To ask about analytics or crash data, email us.
